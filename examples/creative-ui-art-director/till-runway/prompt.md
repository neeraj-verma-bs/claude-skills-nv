Design a desktop personal finance dashboard for working professionals aged 25–35.

The goal is to understand financial health in 5 seconds and then explore spending, investments, bills, and savings.

Do not make it look like a typical fintech dashboard.

Explore 5 genuinely different design directions first. Make them different in layout, visual style, typography, colors, interactions, data visualization, and animations.

Research current UI inspiration from Dribbble, Behance, Figma Community, Awwwards, Mobbin, and similar sources.

Explore styles such as editorial, brutalist, clay, glass, spatial, luxury, cinematic, experimental, or invent new combinations.

Do not just change colors or cards between concepts.

Show the 5 directions, recommend the strongest one, and then develop that direction into the UI.
