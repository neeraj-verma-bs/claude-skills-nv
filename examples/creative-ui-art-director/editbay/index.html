Biography

A1 — TRANSCRIPT
00:00:04:11

Sabine Voss — Sight & Sound, March 2026

00:00:09:02

I came to film through a darkroom, not a film school. My father repaired projectors for three cinemas in North Kolkata and I spent most of my childhood in the booths, which is a strange way to learn a medium — you hear the whole film before you ever see it.

00:00:31:18

On working almost entirely on film stock

00:00:36:05

It isn't nostalgia. It's arithmetic. Four hundred feet is eleven minutes, and when the crew knows that, everyone arrives having already decided something. Scarcity is a director.

00:01:02:23

I moved to Lisbon in 2018 for a job that fell through, and stayed because the light there is the closest thing I've found to the light in the projection booth — low, side-on, and always leaving.

00:01:24:09

On the eleven-person crew rule

00:01:29:14

Eleven people can eat at one table. Past that you are no longer making a film, you are administering one. Every film I've finished was made by people who ate together.

00:01:58:00

Selected teaching: Lisbon Theatre & Film School (2022–), FAMU Prague guest unit (2024). Member, Imago — European Federation of Cinematographers.
